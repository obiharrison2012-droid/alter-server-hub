Serving well

Be prepared

Arrive on time, learn the order of the Mass, and check your vestments and assigned duties before the celebration.

Be reverent

Move calmly, keep a prayerful posture, listen carefully, and avoid distracting others during the liturgy.

Follow local guidance

Never guess what to do with sacred vessels or the liturgical books. Ask your priest, sacristan, or serving coordinator.
